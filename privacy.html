ApolloQLabs Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ApolloQLabs ("ApolloQLabs," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the ApolloQLabs web app (the "App"). It is a free tool for learning about options and analyzing them.

ApolloQLabs is a free, ad-supported tool for learning about options and analyzing them. It includes Markets, Dashboard, Journal, Simulator, Learn, Trends, Technicals, Tools, Options Flow Intelligence and Strategy Finder tabs. ApolloQLabs is not a broker-dealer or investment adviser. The App does not place trades or orders, does not hold or move money or securities, and does not connect to brokerage accounts.

1. Summary

  • Accounts are optional. You can use every part of the App except the Journal as a guest without creating an account. (Opening the site itself may require a separate site password. It is checked in your browser, is not an account, and is not sent to us.) If you choose to create a free account, we collect your email address and a password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication) so we can store your private Journal, save your Simulator practice account and record when you complete the Simulator. We never ask for your name, phone number or postal address. [LEGAL REVIEW]
  • Your data stays on your device. Your practice-account (Simulator) data, course progress, alerts, settings and any optional API key are stored locally in your browser. We do not operate a server that receives them. The only exceptions are for members who create an optional account: the members-only Journal is stored privately in the member's own account, and a member's Simulator practice account is also saved there (see Section 3A). [LEGAL REVIEW]
  • Optional API key. If you choose to enter your own market-data API key, it is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. It is sent only from your device directly to the data provider it belongs to.
  • Advertising. The App is free because it shows ads in clearly labelled "Sponsored" slots. These are first-party placements that we manage ourselves. The App does not use an advertising SDK or ad network, and does not use ad-tracking identifiers. See Section 5.
  • Your choices. You can delete all locally stored data at any time by using the in-app controls described in the App, by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app. If you created an optional account, you can delete it, your Journal and your saved Simulator data yourself under Account → Delete account. Ads are not personalized, so there is nothing to opt out of.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store on our own systems:

  • names, phone numbers or postal addresses;
  • account credentials or email addresses, unless you choose to create an optional account (see Section 3A);
  • your watchlists, settings or Learn progress (journal entries are stored only for signed-in members, in their own account; see Section 3A);
  • your optional API key;
  • brokerage, bank, payment card or other financial-account information;
  • precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data;
  • health, biometric or other sensitive personal information.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. See Section 9.

4. Optional API key and third-party data providers

You may optionally enter your own personal API key for a supported market-data provider (currently Alpha Vantage and Finnhub). If you do:

  • the key is stored only on your device and is never sent to ApolloQLabs servers;
  • when the App needs data from that provider, your device connects directly to the provider over HTTPS and sends the key and the requested symbol, as the provider requires;
  • the provider receives your IP address and request details and handles them under its own terms and privacy policy (Alpha Vantage · Finnhub);
  • you are responsible for complying with the provider's terms for your key.

You can remove the key at any time in Tools → Your own data key → Remove.

Embedded content and public data sources

To show market information, the App connects your device directly to third-party services, which receive your IP address and standard technical information (such as device and browser type) and may set their own cookies or similar technologies inside their embedded frames:

  • TradingView widgets (charts, quotes, screeners, news, calendars) – TradingView privacy policy;
  • U.S. Department of the Treasury (interest-rate data) – Treasury privacy policy;
  • U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) EDGAR (public filings) – SEC privacy policy;
  • Nasdaq (15-minute delayed option-chain snapshots refreshed for the default watchlist);
  • Kenneth R. French Data Library (historical academic factor data bundled at build time).

We do not control these services and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Hosting

The web version of the App is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like most web hosts, it may automatically log standard request information (such as IP address, requested page, date/time and browser type) for security and operations, governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement. ApolloQLabs does not operate analytics trackers or receive server request logs directly.

5. Advertising

Sponsored slots

Ads appear only in slots marked "Sponsored." Sponsored placements are first-party: they are links or banners that we place ourselves, for example for brokers, research services or education providers. The App does not load an ad network, ad SDK or third-party ad script, and the ad content is bundled with the App. The App does not track which ads you see or click, and it does not build an advertising profile of you. If a sponsored link is an affiliate link, we may be paid a commission when you click and later open an account or buy something. Clicking a sponsored link takes you to the partner's own site, where the partner's privacy policy applies. We do not share your journal, positions or other in-App content (including any account data) with advertisers.

No personalized advertising. Because the App does not use an advertising SDK, it does not request or use your device advertising identifier, and there is no ad-personalization setting or consent prompt. If this changes, we will update this policy before it takes effect.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal bases for processing are:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) where you choose to enter an optional API key or turn on optional features that use it;
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing first-party sponsored placements, preventing abuse, and keeping the App and web host secure, as long as these interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • Performance of a contract / providing the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)) for fetching data you ask the App to display and, if you create an optional account, for providing the account, the members-only Journal and Simulator cloud save. [LEGAL REVIEW]

7. Retention

  • On-device data stays on your device until you delete it or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • ApolloQLabs does not keep a copy of your on-device App data. If you create an optional account, your account data (Section 3A) is kept until you ask us to delete the account, plus a limited period for backups and security logs [LEGAL REVIEW: period to be confirmed]. [LEGAL REVIEW]
  • Third parties (TradingView, public data sources, the data provider for any key you enter, and our web host) keep data according to their own policies.

8. Security

The App connects to third-party services over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because your practice account, settings and API key are stored in your browser (and a member's Journal is reached by signing in on it), their security also depends on your device's security (for example, your passcode, browser profile and operating-system updates). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
