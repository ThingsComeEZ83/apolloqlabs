ApolloQLabs Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ApolloQLabs (legal entity name undecided / pending formation; "ApolloQLabs," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the ApolloQLabs app for iOS, iPadOS and Android and the ApolloQLabs web app (together, the "App").

ApolloQLabs is a free, ad-supported tool for learning about options and analyzing them. It includes Markets, Dashboard, Journal, Learn, Trends, Technical Analysis, Tools, and Options Flow Intelligence tabs. ApolloQLabs is not a broker-dealer or investment adviser. The App does not place trades or orders, does not hold or move money or securities, and does not connect to brokerage accounts.

1. Summary

  • No accounts. You do not create an account or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address to use the App.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your journal entries, watchlists, settings and any optional API key are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives them.
  • Optional API key. If you choose to enter your own market-data API key, it is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. It is sent only from your device directly to the data provider it belongs to.
  • Advertising. The App is free because it shows ads in clearly labelled "Sponsored" slots. In the iOS and Android apps, some ads may be served by Google AdMob, which collects certain device and usage information as described in Section 5.
  • Your choices. You can decline tracking (iOS), opt out of personalized ads (Android and iOS), withdraw consent where required (EEA/UK/Switzerland), and delete all locally stored data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store on our own systems:

  • account credentials, names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses;
  • your journal entries, saved trades, positions, watchlists or notes;
  • your optional API key;
  • brokerage, bank, payment card or other financial-account information;
  • precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data;
  • health, biometric or other sensitive personal information.

We do not sell your personal information for money. See Section 9 for how U.S. state laws treat advertising-related "sharing."

3. Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following in your device's local storage so that the App works between sessions:

DataPurposeWhere it is kept
Journal entries and saved (hypothetical) tradesLet you review your own notes and trade ideasYour device only
Settings and preferences (for example, selected symbols, display choices, consent choices)Remember how you set up the AppYour device only
Optional market-data API keyFetch data from the provider you chose, under your own personal licence with that providerYour device only

We cannot see, access, back up or recover this data. If you delete the App, clear its storage, or (for the API key) tap Remove, the data is deleted from your device. Your device's operating system may include App data in your own device backups (for example, iCloud or Google backups) according to your device settings; those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by us.

4. Optional API key and third-party data providers

You may optionally enter your own personal API key for a supported market-data provider (currently Alpha Vantage and Finnhub). If you do:

  • the key is stored only on your device and is never sent to ApolloQLabs servers;
  • when the App needs data from that provider, your device connects directly to the provider over HTTPS and sends the key and the requested symbol, as the provider requires;
  • the provider receives your IP address and request details and handles them under its own terms and privacy policy (Alpha Vantage · Finnhub);
  • you are responsible for complying with the provider's terms for your key.

You can remove the key at any time in Tools → Your own data key → Remove.

Embedded content and public data sources

To show market information, the App connects your device directly to third-party services, which receive your IP address and standard technical information (such as device and browser type) and may set their own cookies or similar technologies inside their embedded frames:

  • TradingView widgets (charts, quotes, screeners, news, calendars) – TradingView privacy policy;
  • U.S. Department of the Treasury (interest-rate data) – Treasury privacy policy;
  • U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) EDGAR (public filings) – SEC privacy policy;
  • Nasdaq (15-minute delayed option-chain snapshots refreshed for the default watchlist);
  • Kenneth R. French Data Library (historical academic factor data bundled at build time).

We do not control these services and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Hosting

The web version of the App is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like most web hosts, it may automatically log standard request information (such as IP address, requested page, date/time and browser type) for security and operations, governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement. ApolloQLabs does not operate analytics trackers or receive server request logs directly.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Sponsored slots

Ads appear only in slots marked "Sponsored." Some sponsored placements are direct links to partners (for example, brokers, research services or education providers). Some of these are affiliate links: if you click and later open an account or buy something, we may be paid a commission. Clicking a sponsored link takes you to the partner's own site or app, where the partner's privacy policy applies. We do not share your journal, positions or other in-App content with advertisers, and we do not let advertisers target ads based on them.

Google AdMob (iOS and Android apps)

The iOS and Android apps may use Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC (and Google Ireland Limited for EEA/UK users, where applicable), to show ads in sponsored slots that we have not sold directly. According to Google's published SDK disclosures, the SDK may automatically collect and share, for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • Device identifiers: the device's advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking; or the Android Advertising ID), app set ID or other app- or developer-scoped identifiers;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (coarse) location;
  • Usage and advertising data: app launches, taps, ad views and clicks, and which ads you have seen;
  • Diagnostic and performance data: crash logs, app launch time, hang rate and energy use.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google states this data is encrypted in transit. Google's disclosures: iOS · Android.

Ad mediation partners: none.

Advertising identifiers and your choices

  • iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency). We ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the App or AdMob accesses your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) or tracks you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," the IDFA is not accessed and you will see non-personalized or contextual ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The App works the same whether or not you allow tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). When you opt out, the advertising ID is not used to build advertising profiles or to show you personalized ads; it may still be used for contextual ads, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention as Google's policies permit.
  • Personalized vs. non-personalized ads. Where we offer it, you can choose non-personalized ads in the App at your device privacy settings (and Tools in the web app). Non-personalized ads are based on context (such as the App's content and your general location) rather than your past behaviour.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message, using Google's User Messaging Platform or another certified consent management platform, before AdMob uses cookies, device identifiers or other local storage for advertising or processes your personal data for personalized ads. You can accept, reject or manage your choices, and you can change or withdraw consent at any time at your device privacy settings or the consent prompt. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing before withdrawal.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal bases for processing are:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalized advertising, access to device identifiers for advertising, and related storage on your device;
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized ads, measuring ad delivery, preventing fraud, and keeping the App and web host secure, as long as these interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • Performance of a contract / providing the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)) for fetching data you ask the App to display.

7. Retention

  • On-device data stays on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • ApolloQLabs does not keep a copy of your App data.
  • Third parties (Google AdMob, TradingView, data providers, our web host) keep data according to their own policies.

8. Security

The App connects to third-party services over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because your journal, settings and API key are stored on your device, their security also depends on your device's security (for example, your passcode and operating-system updates). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Your privacy rights

Everyone

Because we do not hold an account or a copy of your App data, the fastest way to access, export or delete your data is on your device: review it in the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. You can also contact us using the details in Section 13 with any question or request.

California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" of personal information, the "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and "targeted advertising."

  • Categories collected in the last 12 months (by our advertising partner through the App, if AdMob is enabled): identifiers (advertising ID, IP address); internet or other electronic network activity (ad interactions, app interactions); approximate geolocation derived from IP address. Sources: your device. Purpose: advertising, measurement, fraud prevention. Disclosed to: Google .
  • Sale/sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. Allowing AdMob to show personalized ads may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. To opt out, choose non-personalized ads at your device ad settings, deny tracking on iOS, or opt out of ads personalization on Android. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals in the web version where technically supported.
  • Sensitive personal information. We do not collect it.
  • Non-discrimination. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.
  • How to submit a request: contact us using the details in Section 13. We may need to verify your request; since we do not hold accounts, we may be unable to link most requests to any data we hold. You may use an authorized agent as permitted by law.
  • Minors. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

EEA, UK and Swiss residents

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us using Section 13.

International transfers

ApolloQLabs is based in Chicago, Illinois, USA. Our advertising and service partners may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses used by those partners.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA), and it is not intended for use by minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is not listed in Apple's Kids Category or Google Play's Families program. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us using the details in Section 13 and we will take appropriate steps, including asking our advertising partner to delete it where possible.

11. Links to other sites

The App contains links to third-party websites and apps, including sponsored and affiliate links, SEC and Treasury pages, and TradingView. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, give notice in the App or on our website before the change takes effect. Material changes to how personal data is used will require your consent where the law requires it.
